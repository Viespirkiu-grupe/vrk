# Third-party assets

`viespirkiai.svg` and `icon.svg` are unmodified assets from
[Viešpirkiai](https://github.com/Viespirkiu-grupe/viespirkiai), revision
`d445130107984313e67e317edae58301596bce5f`. The reference repository distributes
these assets under AGPL-3.0; its license is retained in `LICENSE-AGPL-3.0.txt`.
Their editable SVG sources are included here. These assets are not relicensed
under the VRK repository's MIT license.

The Ubuntu fonts in `frontend/src/assets/fonts/` are unmodified Latin and Latin extended subsets
from the same reference site. They are distributed under the Ubuntu Font
Licence 1.0, retained in `../fonts/UFL.txt`.

VRK's Astro components and styles are new implementations of the shared visual
language and remain under the repository's MIT license.
